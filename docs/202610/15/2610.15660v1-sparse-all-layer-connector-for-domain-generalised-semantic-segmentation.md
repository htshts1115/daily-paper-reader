## 摘要
领域泛化语义分割（DGSS）近来受益于结合多个基础模型，例如视觉-语言模型和视觉基础模型，以利用其多样化的表征来提升泛化能力。现有多模型方法有一个共同的设计选择：跨模型融合被限制在深度对齐的层之间，隐含地假设互益信息位于不同模型的相同深度处。然而，在不同目标下预训练的基础模型可能形成不同的表征层级，这使得深度对齐融合的最优性值得怀疑。此外，我们观察到这些基础模型表现出不同的逐深度领域敏感性。受这些观察启发，我们提出稀疏全层连接器（SALC），使每一层能够访问另一模型先前层的信息。随着可访问层数随深度增加，稠密聚合可能损害泛化。因此，SALC 学习自适应地选择信息量大的层的稀疏子集。我们进一步引入候选 dropout 正则化，以增强稀疏性并鼓励 SALC 探索多样化的层，从而实现更稳健的选择。在四种基础模型组合和三种 DGSS 评估设置下，所提出的设计表现出比传统深度对齐融合更强的泛化能力。代码可在 https://github.com/RuoyuGuo/SALC 获取。

## Abstract
Domain generalised semantic segmentation (DGSS) has recently benefited from combining multiple foundation models, such as vision-language and vision foundation models, to leverage their diverse representations for generalisation. Existing multi-model approaches share a common design choice: cross-model fusion is restricted to depth-aligned layers, implicitly assuming that mutually beneficial information resides at the same depth across models. However, foundation models pretrained under different objectives may develop distinct representation hierarchies, making the optimality of depth-aligned fusion questionable. Moreover, we observe that the foundation models exhibit different depth-wise domain sensitivity. Motivated by these observations, we propose a Sparse All-Layer Connector (SALC) that enables each layer to access information from preceding layers of another model. As the number of accessible layers grows with depth, dense aggregation may hurt generalisation. SALC therefore learns to adaptively select a sparse subset of informative layers. We further introduce a candidate dropout regularisation that strengthens sparsity and encourages SALC to explore diverse layers, leading to more robust selection. Across four foundation model combinations and three DGSS evaluation settings, the proposed design demonstrates stronger generalisation than conventional depth-aligned fusion. The code is available at https://github.com/RuoyuGuo/SALC.
