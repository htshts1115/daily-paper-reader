## 摘要
频域信息有助于语义分割。然而，现有的基于频率的方法往往存在频率利用不充分、融合效率低和计算复杂度高的问题。为解决这些问题，我们提出 FGD2HF，一种利用频率特征引导空间域融合与增强的双域融合网络。具体而言，我们引入一种新颖的混合注意力编码器（NAHA），通过邻域注意力和超图注意力联合建模局部与全局关系，增强网络提取有效特征的能力。我们进一步引入小波变换与增强（WTE）模块，从上一阶段特征中提取并细化低频和高频线索，随后由频率引导的双域融合（FGDF）模块利用频率线索来调节当前阶段的空间分组与特征传播。该设计以较低计算成本将全局和局部频率引导融入空间特征调制、增强与融合。与众多最先进语义分割方法的广泛比较表明，我们的方法极具竞争力。完整的 FGD2HF-B 模型在 ADE20K 上达到 48.7% mIoU（比 SegMAN-B 高 0.3%，且 GFLOPs 少 18%），在 Cityscapes 上达到 83.2% mIoU（与 SegMAN-B 相当，且 GFLOPs 少 21%），在 SBD 上比 VWFormer-T 高 1.1% mIoU（且 GFLOPs 少 21%），同时在边界分割精度上也取得显著提升。

## Abstract
Frequency-domain information is beneficial for semantic segmentation. However, existing frequency-based methods often suffer from insufficient frequency utilization, inefficient fusion, and high computational complexity. To address these issues, we propose FGD2HF, a dual-domain fusion network that leverages frequency features to guide spatial-domain fusion and enhancement. Specifically, we introduce a novel Hybrid-Attention Encoder (NAHA) that jointly models local and global relationships through neighborhood and hypergraph attention, enhancing the network's ability to extract effective features. We further introduce a Wavelet Transform and Enhancement (WTE) module to extract and refine low- and high-frequency cues from preceding-stage features, followed by Frequency-guided Dual-domain Fusion (FGDF) modules that use frequency cues to condition current-stage spatial grouping and feature propagation. This design incorporates both global and local frequency guidance into spatial feature modulation, enhancement and fusion with low computational cost. Extensive comparisons with numerous state-of-the-art semantic segmentation methods demonstrate that our method is highly competitive. Our full FGD2HF-B model achieves 48.7% mIoU on ADE20K (+0.3% over SegMAN-B with 18% fewer GFLOPs), 83.2% mIoU on Cityscapes (comparable to SegMAN-B with 21% fewer GFLOPs), and 1.1% higher mIoU than VWFormer-T on SBD with 21% fewer GFLOPs, while also achieving significant improvements in boundary segmentation accuracy.
