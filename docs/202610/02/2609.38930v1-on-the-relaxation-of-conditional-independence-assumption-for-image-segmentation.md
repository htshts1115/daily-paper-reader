## 摘要
在语义分割中，近期一系列 RankSEG 方法在推理时直接优化 Dice/IoU 分数，无需修改模型训练即可提升与评估指标的一致性。尽管在理论和实证上取得了成功，RankSEG 仍依赖于限制性较强的条件独立性假设（CIA），该假设忽略了关键的标签相关性，因而在模糊或低对比度场景中会导致性能下降。然而，考虑完整的标签依赖性在计算上不可行，需要 O(d^3) 时间。为解决这一问题，我们用空间局部依赖（SLD）结构替代 CIA，该结构能够捕获局部标签相关性，同时使依赖模型保持可处理。我们进一步通过倒数矩近似结合一种新颖的不动点优化策略来克服剩余的计算瓶颈，从而消除穷举搜索。所提算法实现了非常实用的 O(d log d) 复杂度，并在多种分割基准上持续优于传统 argmax 和基于 CIA 的 RankSEG。在低对比度或小目标场景中提升显著，此时标签依赖性提供了与图像信息互补的宝贵信号，有助于实现准确分割。实验代码见 https://github.com/ZixunWang/RankSEG-DEP。

## Abstract
In semantic segmentation, a recent line of RankSEG methods directly optimizes Dice/IoU scores at inference time, improving alignment with evaluation metrics without modifying model training. Despite its theoretical and empirical success, RankSEG relies on the restrictive Conditional Independence Assumption (CIA), which ignores crucial label correlations and therefore degrades performance in ambiguous or low-contrast scenarios. However, accounting for full label dependence is computationally prohibitive, requiring $\mathcal{O}(d^3)$ time. To address this, we replace the CIA with a Spatially Localized Dependence (SLD) structure that captures local label correlations while keeping the dependence model tractable. We further overcome the remaining computational bottleneck via a Reciprocal Moment Approximation coupled with a novel fixed-point optimization strategy that eliminates exhaustive search. The proposed algorithm achieves a highly practical $\mathcal{O}(d \log d)$ complexity and consistently outperforms conventional argmax and CIA-based RankSEG across diverse segmentation benchmarks. Improvements are significant in low-contrast or small-object scenarios, where label dependence offers valuable signals complementary to image information for accurate segmentation. The code of experiments is available at https://github.com/ZixunWang/RankSEG-DEP.
