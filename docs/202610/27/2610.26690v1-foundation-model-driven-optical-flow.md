## 摘要
我们研究冻结的预训练视觉与深度表示在与有监督匹配网络耦合时如何支持稠密光流。本文在 TTSFlow 的基础上，研究稠密预测 Transformer（DPT）特征解码以及一个由粗到细的细化阶段。我们的方法融合来自冻结的 DINOv2 与 Depth Anything V2 模型的特征，并通过可训练的基于 Transformer 的匹配流水线估计光流。在 FlyingChairs 和 FlyingThings3D 上完成光流训练后，该方法在 Sintel 训练划分的 Final pass 上达到 2.37 的端点误差（EPE），而本文报告的 FlowSeek（T）配置为 2.52。我们的 RoCo-Spring 2026 参赛方案 RoCo-29.VSAI 获得 1.1038 的鲁棒性平衡分数、1.411 的 Spring EPE 和 4.739 的 RobustSpring 代理 EPE，其平衡分数低于所列十二个参考基线中的七个。这些结果刻画了完整流水线，该流水线复用冻结的基础模型骨干并执行一个细化阶段。代码见：\url{https://github.com/Visual-and-Spatial-AI-Lab/FFlow}

## Abstract
We investigate how frozen pretrained visual and depth representations support dense optical flow when coupled with a supervised matching network. Building on TTSFlow, this paper studies Dense Prediction Transformer (DPT) feature decoding and one coarse-to-fine refinement stage. Our method fuses features from frozen DINOv2 and Depth Anything V2 models and estimates optical flow through a trainable transformer-based matching pipeline. After optical-flow training on FlyingChairs and FlyingThings3D, it achieves 2.37 endpoint error (EPE) on the Sintel training split, Final pass, compared with 2.52 for the FlowSeek (T) configuration reported here. Our RoCo-Spring 2026 entry, RoCo-29.VSAI, obtains a robustness-balanced score of 1.1038, Spring EPE of 1.411, and RobustSpring proxy EPE of 4.739, with a lower balanced score than seven of the twelve listed reference baselines. These results characterize the complete pipeline, which reuses frozen foundation backbones and performs one refinement stage. Code is available at: \url{https://github.com/Visual-and-Spatial-AI-Lab/FFlow}
