## 摘要
通用图像编辑器常被要求让照片看起来像是在 f/1.4 下拍摄的，但人们很少检查它们所添加的模糊是否符合薄透镜光学。物理光圈编辑会按照薄透镜比例在深度上分布模糊，并在光圈变化时改变模糊；此前的评估检查模糊单调性、清晰度趋势相关性、有效光圈误差或视觉-语言判断，而我们未发现有任何评估在已知深度上分别报告这两种特性。在这项针对两个编辑器（Gemini~3.1 Flash Image 和 GPT-image-2.5）的试点审计中，我们与一个渲染 oracle 进行比较：使用具有真实薄透镜景深的 Blender Cycles 场景，对 oracle 和编辑器输出以相同方式应用同一个模糊宽度估计器，并使用预注册的深度和光圈指标。在 24 个以同一种三面板几何结构渲染的纹理场景中，被接受且可测量的面板显示，f/1.4 到 f/2.8 的宽度比 $\barσ_{1.4}/\barσ_{2.8}$ 为 0.99--1.18，而 oracle 为 1.98--2.13；合并中位高斯等效近/远模糊宽度之比约为 1.18--1.25（Gemini）和 0.97--1.05（GPT-image），而 oracle 为 1.69--1.82。预注册的黑箱干预表明，定性措辞会使模糊强度改变约 2--10 倍，而请求 2 px 与 6 px 仅使其改变 1.1--1.3 倍，并且没有任何受测的 f 值措辞满足预注册的“遵循”标准。这种深度压缩在原始和反转的中心对焦布局中均出现；当焦点位于近处面板时，可用面板的深度指标达到预注册阈值，而光圈响应在所有测试布局中始终减弱。从已发表指标改编而来的标量指标会给比例被压缩的合成编辑器打出类似 oracle 的分数。

## Abstract
General image editors are asked to make a photo look as if it were taken at f/1.4, yet it is rarely checked whether the blur they add follows thin-lens optics. A physical aperture edit spreads blur across depth in thin-lens proportions and changes the blur when the aperture changes; prior evaluations check blur monotonicity, sharpness-trend correlation, effective-aperture error, or vision-language judgments, and none we found reports the two properties separately at known depths. In this pilot audit of two editors (Gemini~3.1 Flash Image and GPT-image-2.5) we compare against a rendered oracle: Blender Cycles scenes with true thin-lens depth of field, one blur-width estimator applied identically to oracle and editor outputs, and preregistered depth and aperture indices. In 24 texture scenes rendered in one three-panel geometry, accepted and measurable panels show f/1.4-to-f/2.8 width ratios, $\barσ_{1.4}/\barσ_{2.8}$, of 0.99--1.18 against 1.98--2.13 for the oracle, and ratios of pooled median Gaussian-equivalent near/far blur widths of about 1.18--1.25 (Gemini) and 0.97--1.05 (GPT-image) against 1.69--1.82. Preregistered black-box interventions show that qualitative wording changes blur strength by roughly 2--10 times, whereas a request for 2 versus 6 px changes it 1.1--1.3 times and no tested wording of the f-number meets the registered ``followed'' criterion. The depth compression appears in the original and reversed centre-focus layouts; with the focus on the near panel, the available-panel depth index reaches the registered threshold, and the aperture response stays attenuated in every layout tested. Scalar metrics adapted from published ones give oracle-like scores to synthetic editors whose proportions are compressed.
