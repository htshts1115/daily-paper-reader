## 摘要
现有的语义分割知识蒸馏（KD）方法通常引导学生模仿教师网络从单张图像生成的结构化信息。然而，它们忽略了不同图像之间对知识蒸馏有价值的全局语义关系。本文提出跨图像关系知识蒸馏 V2（CIRKDV2），重点迁移从数据集全局图像中建模的像素到像素、像素到区域以及通道到通道的结构化关系。大型教师网络通常能够基于全局上下文依赖构建结构良好的特征空间。CIRKDV2 使学生模仿教师网络更好的结构化语义关系，从而提升特征表示能力。除关系蒸馏外，我们进一步提出上下文特征蒸馏，通过上下文聚合增强学生像素嵌入，使其语义层次与教师网络相匹配。这避免了传统特征蒸馏研究中出现的教师与学生之间的语义鸿沟问题。在 Cityscapes、ADE20K、Pascal VOC 和 COCO-Stuff-164K 上的实验结果表明，CIRKDV2 在各种网络架构上大幅优于最先进的语义分割蒸馏方法。我们的代码和预训练模型已公开于 https://github.com/winycg/CIRKD。

## Abstract
Existing Knowledge Distillation (KD) methods for semantic segmentation often guide the student to mimic the teacher's structured information generated from an individual image. However, they ignore the global semantic relations across various images that are valuable for KD. This paper proposes Cross-Image Relational KD V2 (CIRKDV2), which focuses on transferring structured pixel-to-pixel, pixel-to-region, and channel-to-channel relations modeled from global images in the dataset. A large teacher network often constructs a well-structured feature space in terms of global contextual dependencies. CIRKDV2 makes the student mimic the better structured semantic relations of the teacher, thus improving the feature representational capability. Beyond relational distillation, we further propose contextual feature distillation by using context aggregation to augment student pixel embeddings to match the semantic level with the teacher. This avoids the semantic gap problem between the teacher and student occurred in traditional feature distillation studies. Experimental results on Cityscapes, ADE20K, Pascal VOC, and COCO-Stuff-164K demonstrate that CIRKDV2 outperforms the state-of-the-art semantic segmentation distillation methods by large margins across various network architectures. Our code and pre-trained models are publicly available at https://github.com/winycg/CIRKD.
